# Utangard - fighting in a locked biome earns eating back there, and only there. LHM-26.
#
# What it proves. In the Deep North with no foothold, a bite is refused, the meat stays in the
# pack, and the refusal says how far the Fighting bar has got. A Jotun warrior killed in god mode
# goes on the game's own kill record and not on Utangard's: the game marks a creature hit by a
# player in god mode as cheated, Utangard refuses those, and it refuses your own kill while you
# are in god mode as well. Utangard's own count fills the bar by points: a Jotun warrior is worth
# 5 of a 150-point bar. At 14 warriors the bar is 70 points, which is 46%, and the bite is still
# refused. At 15 it is 75, which is 50%, and the same meat goes down. One kind stops at 75 however
# many more are counted. Then in the Ashlands, which is locked too and where nothing has been
# fought, the bite is refused again. A foothold lifts the rule only in the biome it was earned in.
#
# The warriors are counted with `mod utangardtest kills`, not fought. Fifteen Jotun warriors in
# the Deep North with god mode off could kill the tester, and which kills count is already tested
# in utangard-foothold-kills. This file asks what the count does to eating. The god-mode kill
# below is here because it is the kill a tester makes without thinking.
#
# What it needs. A world you host. Default Foothold settings: FightingFullPoints 150,
# EatAtFightingPercent 50, MaxFromOneKindPercent 50, and a Points_DeepNorth line that pays
# JotunWarrior 5. BlockEating on. The Deep North and the Ashlands are shut by clearing Fader's and
# the Queen's records, the same five keys each that utangard-altar-in-a-locked-biome clears.
# Another character in this world's roster who is marked present at either boss would open it
# again, so if the first `printed` fails, look there first.
#
# What it leaves behind, and none of it can be undone:
#  - Utangard's count, in this world, of every creature on the Deep North and Ashlands points
#    lines: 0 for all of them except JotunWarrior, which is left at 16. It is kept in the
#    character, one entry per world, and the old numbers are not kept anywhere.
#  - The game's own kill record for JotunWarrior, set to 0 by `tally` and left at 1. That record
#    lives in the character and follows it into every world. Use a throwaway character.
#  - The Queen's and Fader's records in this world stay cleared, because the run cannot know
#    whether they were there. `key defeated_queen` and `key defeated_fader` put them back.
#
# `kill` hits from you, so vanilla marks you on the victim and credits you through
# RPC_RegisterKill exactly as a sword would. It holds until the body is gone, which is after the
# game has credited the kill. `killed` reads that credit on its own, so the kill is known to have
# happened when Utangard's number does not move.

clear
god
ghost

unkey defeated_fader
unkey utangard_open_defeated_fader
unkey wither_open_defeated_fader
unkey utangard_first_defeated_fader
unkey utangard_p_$player_defeated_fader
unkey defeated_queen
unkey utangard_open_defeated_queen
unkey wither_open_defeated_queen
unkey utangard_first_defeated_queen
unkey utangard_p_$player_defeated_queen
wait 1

# Shut, with no foothold: refused, and the meat stays.
biome DeepNorth 20
wait 3

# The ground the warrior below is spawned on, cleared and levelled. Devkit's own stage ran before
# the first step, back where the run started. The Ashlands half spawns nothing, so it has none.
stage
banish all 60
hungry
give CookedMeat:3
console utangard foothold
printed DeepNorth (LOCKED, its rules apply to you now): fighting 0/150 (0%) yes
centre The land will not feed you here no
eat CookedMeat
centre Fighting here 0%. You can eat at 50%. yes
carrying CookedMeat 3

# A kill in god mode. The game has it on record; Utangard does not count it.
spawn JotunWarrior
kill JotunWarrior
killed JotunWarrior 1
console utangard foothold
printed DeepNorth (LOCKED, its rules apply to you now): fighting 0/150 (0%) yes

# 14 warriors is 70 points, 46% rounded down. Still short of half, so still refused.
mod utangardtest kills JotunWarrior 14
console utangard foothold
printed DeepNorth (LOCKED, its rules apply to you now): fighting 70/150 (46%) yes
hungry
eat CookedMeat
centre Fighting here 46%. You can eat at 50%. yes
carrying CookedMeat 3

# 15 is 75 points, exactly half.
mod utangardtest kills JotunWarrior 15
console utangard foothold
printed DeepNorth (LOCKED, its rules apply to you now): fighting 75/150 (50%) yes
printed - eating allowed, yes

# A 16th. One kind may put in half the bar and no more, so the bar does not move.
mod utangardtest kills JotunWarrior 16
console utangard foothold
printed 5pt x16 = 75 (capped at 75, 80 earned) yes
printed DeepNorth (LOCKED, its rules apply to you now): fighting 75/150 (50%) yes

# At half the same meat goes down here. Hungry first, so a refusal could only be the land's.
hungry
eat CookedMeat
centre The land will not feed you here no
carrying CookedMeat 2

# The Ashlands, also shut, where nothing has been fought. The Deep North's 75 does not follow
# you: its line no longer says its rules apply, and the bite is refused on the Ashlands' own 0.
biome AshLands 25
wait 3
banish all 60
hungry
console utangard foothold
printed AshLands (LOCKED, its rules apply to you now): fighting 0/150 (0%) yes
printed DeepNorth (LOCKED): fighting 75/150 (50%) yes
centre The land will not feed you here no
eat CookedMeat
centre Fighting here 0%. You can eat at 50%. yes
carrying CookedMeat 2
