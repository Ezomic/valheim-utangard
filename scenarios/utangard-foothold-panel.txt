# Utangard - the compendium page shows the biome you pick and your two bars there. LHM-26.
#
# What it proves. The compendium opens on the Utangard page drawn as a panel. Clicking Deep North
# in the biome strip shows "The Deep North", with the Fighting bar at the percent the console
# prints and the Discovery bar at the percent the console prints, each in its own box beside its
# own heading. The page keeps up while it is open: a change to the kill count shows within its
# one-second refresh without reopening it. Clicking the Meadows, which is open, shows that biome
# and takes the boxes away. And every text on it has the height its text needs, which the strings
# alone cannot show: see the `tall` steps.
#
# The Fighting bar is seeded here with `mod utangardtest kills`, 6 Jotun warriors for 30 of 150
# points, rather than fought. This is a test of what the page draws. utangard-foothold-eating
# tests how the count fills the bar, and utangard-foothold-kills tests which kills go on it.
#
# What it needs. A world you host, default Foothold settings, Presentation.ShowCompendiumPanel on,
# and the game in English, since the biome names are clicked and read by their English names.
# The Deep North is shut the way utangard-altar-in-a-locked-biome shuts it.
#
# What it leaves behind, and none of it can be undone:
#  - Utangard's count, in this world, of every creature on the Deep North points line: 0 for all
#    of them except JotunWarrior, which is left at 9. It is kept in the character, one entry per
#    world, and the old numbers are not kept anywhere. Use a throwaway character.
#  - The Deep North ground the run lands on is uncovered on this character's map of this world.
#  - Fader's records in this world stay cleared. `key defeated_fader` puts them back.

clear
god
ghost

unkey defeated_fader
unkey utangard_open_defeated_fader
unkey wither_open_defeated_fader
unkey utangard_first_defeated_fader
unkey utangard_p_$player_defeated_fader
wait 1

mod utangardtest kills GoblinDeepNorth 0
mod utangardtest kills Elaking 0
mod utangardtest kills ElakingLantern 0
mod utangardtest kills ElakingMole 0
mod utangardtest kills DvergerDeepNorth 0
mod utangardtest kills Moose 0
mod utangardtest kills ShadowPerson 0
mod utangardtest kills JotunWitch 0
mod utangardtest kills JotunWarriorDualWield 0
mod utangardtest kills Barka 0
mod utangardtest kills JotunWarrior 6

# What the console says, so the page can be held to it.
biome DeepNorth 20
wait 3
console utangard foothold
until 180 Map counted.
printed DeepNorth (LOCKED, its rules apply to you now): fighting 30/150 (20%) yes
note pct DeepNorth \(LOCKED.*discovery \d+/\d+ px \((\d+)%

# The page, on the Deep North. It opens on the group's frontier, which on this world may be an
# earlier biome, so the Deep North is clicked either way.
compendium
wait 1
press Deep North
wait 1
panel The Deep North yes

# Every text as tall as its text needs. On 2026-09-29 the page drew each of these at no height,
# on top of one another, and the steps around these still passed, since every string was there.
tall The Deep North
tall Deep North
tall Fighting
tall Discovery
tall Eating at 50%

beside Fighting | 20% yes
panel Eating at 50% yes
beside Discovery | $pct% yes
panel Healing needs both full yes

# Three more warriors on the count while the page stays open: 45 of 150 is 30%.
mod utangardtest kills JotunWarrior 9
wait 2
beside Fighting | 30% yes

# An open biome: its own name, and no bars.
press Meadows
wait 1
panel The Meadows yes
panel Eating at 50% no
close
