#! nostage
# Utangard - no boss comes to an altar in a biome the group has not earned.
#
# The Deep North, because it is the last biome and the one a dev world is least likely to have
# earned - and the scenario makes sure by clearing every record that could open it: the boss
# key the world falls back on, the latch GateNeverRegresses writes (and its pre-rename wither_
# spelling), the catch-up clock, and this character's own attendance. The one thing it cannot
# clear is another character in this world's roster who is marked present at Fader; if the run
# fails at the first `count`, look there first.
#
# The altar is Eikthyr's, spawned where the Deep North is. The rule reads the biome an altar
# stands in, not which boss it belongs to, so any altar in a locked biome is the test, and
# Eikthyr's is the cheapest offering there is.
#
# Two halves. Shut: the offering is refused, the refusal is said, no boss comes, and the
# trophies are still in the pack. Open: latch the Deep North the way a Fader kill would, and the
# same altar with the same offering brings him. Without the second half a broken altar would
# pass the first.
#
# NO STAGE. The `#! nostage` line at the top turns off the ground Devkit makes ready before the
# first step, and there is no `stage` after `biome` either, because a location wants the world's
# own ground under it. It levels its own pad with the game's older terrain system, and the game
# applies that before the terrain compiler a stage writes to, so on staged ground the stage's
# change is added on top of the pad: where the stage raised the slope a metre, the pad comes out a
# metre above the altar's foot, and where it lowered it, a metre below. On the world's own
# ground the pad is level with the altar, which is what `location` is for.

clear
god
ghost

unkey defeated_fader
unkey utangard_open_defeated_fader
unkey wither_open_defeated_fader
unkey utangard_first_defeated_fader
unkey utangard_p_$player_defeated_fader
wait 1

biome DeepNorth
wait 3
location Eikthyrnir
wait 3
banish Eikthyr 80
give TrophyDeer:4

# Both halves wait 15 seconds before counting, because Eikthyr takes about ten to come. The altar
# queues DelayedSpawnBoss for its m_spawnBossDelay, which is asset data, and this altar's is not
# vanilla's code default of 5. The 2026-09-28 run counted at 8 seconds and found nobody although
# the offering had been taken, and Vandi's summoner scenario the same day brackets the delay
# between 9 and 12 seconds: its first offering had brought nobody at 9 seconds, and its second,
# made at 13, brought a boss between 22 and 25. A count inside the delay proves nothing either
# way, and on the shut half it would pass a broken refusal, whose boss would arrive after it.

# Shut.
centre The land will not answer no
offer TrophyDeer
centre The land will not answer an offering here yes
wait 15
count Eikthyr 0 80
carrying TrophyDeer 4

# Open, the way the group's Fader kill would leave it.
key utangard_open_defeated_fader
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer
centre The land will not answer no
wait 15
count Eikthyr 1 80
carrying TrophyDeer 2

banish Eikthyr 80
unkey utangard_open_defeated_fader
